Provide streamlined review packaging (osr review-pack) and rapid single-command verification (osr quick) for small bug fixes.

## Inputs and context
- osr review-pack outputs pure diffs and criteria without builder rationale.
- osr quick streamlines verification for simple single-file fixes.

## Constraints
- Standard library only.

## Do not
- Do not include model chain-of-thought in review packages.

## Definition of done
- review-pack outputs unbiased context-free diffs.
- osr quick performs rapid verification without task card overhead.
- All unit tests pass.
