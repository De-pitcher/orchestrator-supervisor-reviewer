# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique ideas and evidence, not
people. Assume good faith while making room for disagreement, different
backgrounds and different levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, impersonation,
doxxing and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable.
Do not use issues or reviews for spam, personal attacks or repeated disruption.
Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and describe AI-assisted contributions
honestly; never fabricate review independence or verification results.

These standards apply in project issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project-managed spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close
disruptive threads, restrict participation or report platform abuse. Responses
should consider context and severity and should be explained where doing so
does not expose private information.

## Private conduct reports

Contact the maintainer at [emmanwa000@gmail.com](mailto:emmanwa000@gmail.com).
Include the relevant links and enough context to assess the situation. Do not
post private evidence publicly. Reports are handled on a best-effort basis, with
information shared only as needed to address the concern; there is no promised
response time or separate conduct committee.

Use the [security reporting channel](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities. GitHub's
own reporting and moderation tools remain available for platform abuse.
